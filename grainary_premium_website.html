Sona Masoori Steam Rice

Parboiled Sona Masoori — partially cooked in the husk before milling. This pre-gelatinises the starch, making it easier and faster to cook at home. The parboiling process also drives some surface nutrients into the grain, giving it a nutritional edge over raw rice.

Steam rice cooks faster, is gentler on the digestive system, and is the daily staple of millions of South Indian families. Perfect for everyday meals.

Raw Rice — Idli/Dosa Grade
